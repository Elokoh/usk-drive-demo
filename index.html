(faq)

The questions everyone asks first.

How many lessons will I need?＋

The DVSA average is around 45 hours of professional tuition plus private practice — but most of my pupils are test-ready in 30–40 hours because every lesson follows a structured plan. After your taster I'll give you an honest estimate, not a sales pitch.

Can you pick me up from college or work?＋

Yes — free pickup and drop-off anywhere in the areas listed above, including Coleg Gwent, Newport city centre and the retail parks. Many pupils start their lesson at college and finish at home.

Do you teach automatic as well as manual?＋

Both. The manual car is a Ford Fiesta with dual controls; the automatic is a Hyundai i20. Bear in mind an automatic licence only covers automatics — I'm happy to talk through which suits you.

Can you help with the theory test?＋

Every pupil gets free access to a theory app with all the official DVSA questions and hazard perception clips, plus I'll check your progress and flag weak spots before you book.

What if I need to cancel a lesson?＋

Life happens — give me 48 hours' notice and there's no charge, we'll just rebook. Inside 48 hours the lesson is chargeable, because I can't fill the slot at short notice.
